# RT1: retail domain plug-in

Branch `feat/rt1-retail`, from `bylaw/build` at 3436f63 (contracts v1.1).

## Problem and the rule it breaks

Bylaw had a single domain (airline), so it could not show that it works on a policy we did not write. REQ-RT1-1..3 ask for τ² retail as a second domain: a C1 `Domain`, pre-registered goals, and a hand RuleSet that is valid under C2.

## Root cause

Not a bug fix. The one Class A hole this item found has its root in the simulator:
`.context/tau2-bench/src/tau2/domains/retail/tools.py:218`. `cancel_pending_order` refunds every line of `payment_history`, and that includes the `refund` line that `modify_pending_order_payment` appends at `tools.py:639`. The policy allows both calls on a pending order, so a payment switch followed by a cancel refunds three times what was paid once.

## Change

- `bylaw/goals_retail.py` (pre-registration, its own commit 6986445, made before any chain ran): goals R1-R8 as `(db_before, db_after, chain) -> bool` over snapshot dicts. Each one fires only on orders the chain changed, and each quotes a line of the retail policy. R5 and R8 are Class C; the rest are Class A.
- `bylaw/env/tau2_retail.py`: `make_domain()` returns a C1 `RetailDomain`. Its `snapshot` gives `{"users","orders","products"}` keyed by id (v1.1). `call` drops any arg the τ² tool does not take (v1.1), and `targets(goal)` gives order and user seeds by status. `StepResult` and `Goal` are defined locally, with a TODO to import them from `bylaw.domain` (EN1).
- `bylaw/rulesets/retail.json`: 24 hand rules across the 7 write tools, in the C2 expression language, plus `forbidden_goals` copied from the goals file.
- `spike/retail_holes_by_hand.py` and `.json`: 9 candidate chains run on fresh envs. Each step is checked against retail.json before it runs. A chain that reproduces is replayed 5 times. The file also holds a stand-in C2 checker (AST whitelist plus eval with no builtins), with a TODO to replace it with `bylaw.rules.validate/check` (EN1).

## Tests added

`tests/test_retail.py`, 19 tests:
- env independence, and a snapshot is a copy;
- conversation facts are stripped and rejections come back as data;
- the C1 surface and `targets`;
- every goal is false on the untouched DB and its policy_ref is quoted;
- R1, R2, R3, R4, R6 and R7 each fire on the forbidden outcome and not on the permitted one;
- retail.json C2 shape, only retail write tools, refs quoted, and goals in sync;
- the whitelist rejects imports, dunders, other calls, lambdas and free names;
- negative controls: retail.json blocks the direct one-step routes;
- spike H1 is Class A with 5/5 replays, and no blocked chain is counted.

## Out of scope / not done

- `bylaw/domain.py` and `bylaw/rules.py` are EN1's. Until EN1 lands, `get_domain("retail")` does not exist, and retail.json has been validated only by the local checker, not by `bylaw.rules.validate`.
- The gate does not stamp, on this branch or on `bylaw/build`: `tests/test_spike.py` (airline) rewrites `spike/holes_by_hand.json`, and the diff key order is not deterministic. That is outside this brief.
- The rules cannot see some things:
  - pairing `item_ids` with `new_item_ids` by position needs `zip`, which C2 lacks, so it is checked per new item;
  - an address modification leaves no trace in the order, so "once per order" cannot be enforced for it (this is H2);
  - explicit user confirmation and authentication happen in the conversation.
- Simulator bug, not used by any goal: `modify_pending_order_items` at `tools.py:583` writes the last variant's price and options onto every modified item.
